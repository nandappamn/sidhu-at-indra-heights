# 30-Day Fast-Track Renovation Master Plan (2BHK Apartment)

**Property Profile:** 20+ Year Old Apartment (Empty / Vacated during work)  
**Spaces Covered:** Hall, Kitchen, 2 Bedrooms, 1 Bathroom, 1 Washroom (WC), 1 Open Balcony  
**Target Timeline:** 30 Days  

---

## 1. Pre-Renovation Checklist (15–20 Days Before Day 1)

To complete a full civil, plumbing, electrical, and modular renovation in 30 days, **zero days can be lost waiting for materials or factory production.** Complete these items before demolition begins:

### Society & Logistics
- [ ] **Society NOC:** Keep a printed copy pasted on the main door for security and neighbours.
- [ ] **Working Hours Confirmed:** Inform contractors of strict noisy-work hours (usually 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
- [ ] **Common Area Protection:** Pad the lift and lobby walls with corrugated sheets/gunny bags.
- [ ] **Downstairs Neighbour Check:** Take time-stamped photos of the ceiling of the flat directly below yours (especially below the bathroom, washroom, and kitchen) as a baseline record.
- [ ] **Shut-off Valves Located:** Identify the main water isolation valves in the building's plumbing shaft.
- [ ] **Electricity Load Check:** Apply for a 3-Phase meter upgrade (if currently on 1-Phase and adding multiple ACs/heavy kitchen appliances).

### Pre-Orders & Measurements (Critical for 30-Day Target)
- [ ] **100% Factory Modular Order Placed:** Finalize designs and trigger factory production for the **Modular Kitchen** and **2 Bedroom Wardrobes** 15–20 days *before* Day 1.
- [ ] **Finished Floor Level (FFL) Locked:** Instruct the mason and modular vendor on exact tile thickness (+15 mm if doing Tile-on-Tile) so pre-ordered cabinets fit accurately on Day 22.
- [ ] **All Materials on Site / Ready for Dispatch:**
  - [ ] CPVC pipes, fittings, and concealed diverter/mixer bodies
  - [ ] Fast-curing polymer waterproofing chemicals
  - [ ] Floor & wall tiles + tile-on-tile chemical adhesive + epoxy grout
  - [ ] FRLS copper wires, modular switchboxes, and new MCB Distribution Board
  - [ ] Kitchen chimney, exhaust fan, and rigid PVC/aluminium exhaust duct pipe
  - [ ] Sanitaryware (WC, basin, taps, showerheads) and light fixtures

---

## 2. The 3 Non-Negotiable Rules for a 30-Day Sprint

1. **Zero On-Site Carpentry:** All kitchen cabinets and both bedroom wardrobes must be pre-manufactured in a factory while civil work happens on site, requiring only 4–5 days of assembly in Week 4.
2. **Tile-on-Tile for Rooms + Fast-Cure Waterproofing:** Use chemical tile adhesive over existing floors in the Hall and 2 Bedrooms to save 5 days of floor breaking and mortar curing. Use rapid-setting polymer waterproofing in the Bathroom and Washroom instead of traditional brickbat coba.
3. **Strict Structural Safety:** Never cut deep electrical grooves into RCC columns or beams. Run wires crossing beams inside the false ceiling.

---

## 3. Week-by-Week Execution Schedule (Days 1 to 30)

### Week 1: Demolition, Concealed Plumbing & Electrical (Days 1–7)
*Goal: Strip wet areas, complete all concealed infrastructure inside walls, and seal grooves.*

- [ ] **Days 1–3: Demolition & Debris Removal**
  - Strip out old kitchen platform/tiles.
  - Completely strip wall and floor tiles in the **1 Bathroom** and **1 Washroom**.
  - Bag debris (*malba*) into cement sacks daily; stack along perimeter walls (never pile in the center of the hall slab) and clear out every 2 days.
- [ ] **Days 4–6: Plumbing First Fix & Kitchen Core-Cut**
  - Replace all old 20-year-old metal (GI) pipes in the Bathroom, Washroom, and Kitchen with new **CPVC pipes**.
  - Install concealed wall mixer/diverter bodies and pressure-test the pipes for leaks.
  - Drill the 6-inch **core-cut hole** through the kitchen exterior wall for the chimney exhaust duct.
- [ ] **Days 5–7: Electrical First Fix (Wires & Boards)**
  - Pull fresh FRLS copper wires through conduits across all 4 rooms.
  - Cut minor wall grooves for new kitchen appliance points (16A) and false ceiling drops; replace old metal switch boxes and upgrade the main MCB DB box.
  - **Crucial Step:** Take photos of all open wall grooves (plumbing & electrical) before plastering over them so installers don't drill into pipes later.

---

### Week 2: Waterproofing, False Ceiling & Wet-Area Tiling (Days 8–15)
*Goal: Seal wet areas, hang ceiling grids + exhaust ducting, and finish bathroom/kitchen tiling.*

- [ ] **Days 8–10: Waterproofing & Ponding Test**
  - Plaster all plumbing/electrical wall grooves using chicken mesh.
  - Apply 2 coats of fast-curing polymer waterproofing in the Bathroom, Washroom, Kitchen sink zone, and around the Open Balcony drain trap.
  - Flood Bathroom and Washroom with water for 24–36 hours and verify **zero dampness** in the flat below.
- [ ] **Days 8–12: False Ceiling Framework & Exhaust Ducting (Parallel Work)**
  - Install GI metal framing for the gypsum false ceiling in the Hall and 2 Bedrooms.
  - Install and conceal the **rigid kitchen exhaust duct pipe** up to the exterior core-cut hole.
  - Drop electrical wires for COB/spotlights and ceiling fans, then screw on the gypsum boards.
- [ ] **Days 11–15: Bathroom, Washroom & Kitchen Tiling**
  - Lay wall tiles (up to ceiling) and anti-skid floor tiles (with proper slope to drain) in the Bathroom and Washroom.
  - Set up the Kitchen granite countertop (if civil) and lay the 2-foot kitchen dado wall tiles.

---

### Week 3: Main Flooring, Wall Putty & Base Primer (Days 16–21)
*Goal: Complete all dusty and wet civil work so the flat is 100% dry and sealed for carpentry.*

- [ ] **Days 14–18: 4-Room Floor Tiling**
  - Lay floor tiles across the Hall, Kitchen, Master Bedroom, and 2nd Bedroom using high-strength tile adhesive.
  - Trim or adjust main door and balcony door bottoms if floor height increased by ~15 mm.
  - **Floor Protection:** Immediately cover the entire new floor with heavy corrugated rolls or plastic sheets + POP.
- [ ] **Days 18–21: Ceiling Taping, Wall Putty & Primer**
  - Tape and compound all false ceiling gypsum joints.
  - Apply 2 coats of fast-drying wall putty across the Hall and 2 Bedrooms.
  - Sand the walls smooth and apply **1 base coat of wall primer** everywhere to lock in masonry dust.

---

### Week 4: Modular Installation, Final Paint & Handover (Days 22–30)
*Goal: Assemble factory woodwork, apply finish coats, clip on electrical/plumbing fittings, and clean.*

- [ ] **Days 22–26: Modular Kitchen & Wardrobe Assembly**
  - Deliver factory-made carcasses and shutters to the flat.
  - **Team A (Kitchen):** Assemble under-counter tandem drawers, overhead cabinets, tall unit, and accessories.
  - **Team B (Bedrooms):** Assemble the 2 wardrobes (1 in each bedroom) along with lofts and hardware.
- [ ] **Days 25–27: Main Paint Coats & Open Balcony Refresh**
  - Apply 2 coats of interior emulsion paint on the false ceilings and walls of the Hall and 2 Bedrooms.
  - Apply weather-proof exterior emulsion in the **Open Balcony** and enamel paint on the balcony safety railing.
- [ ] **Days 28–29: Electrical & Plumbing "Second Fix" (Final Fittings)**
  - **Electrical:** Clip on new modular switch plates/sockets, hang ceiling fans, fit false ceiling lights, and mount the **Kitchen Chimney & Exhaust Fan**.
  - **Plumbing:** Install the WC in the Washroom, washbasin/taps/shower/geyser in the Bathroom, and sink + RO connections in the Kitchen.
- [ ] **Day 30: Deep Cleaning & Final Paint Touch-Up**
  - Remove all floor protection sheets and clean tile joints/grout.
  - Perform a full professional deep cleaning of rooms, wardrobes, kitchen cabinets, bathroom, washroom, and balcony.
  - Have one painter spend half a day doing final **touch-up paint** over any installation scuffs or fingerprints.
